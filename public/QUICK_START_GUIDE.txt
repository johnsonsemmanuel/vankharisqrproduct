VANKHARIS QR & BARCODE QUICK START GUIDE
=========================================

FOR CUSTOMERS
-------------
1. Scan QR code with your phone camera
2. View product info, recipes & verify authenticity
3. Leave feedback if desired

FOR RETAILERS
-------------
1. Use EAN-13 barcode at POS checkout
2. Scan QR to verify authenticity if needed

FOR STAFF
---------
1. Create batches in Admin > Batch Registry
2. Generate QR codes in Admin > QR Studio
3. Generate barcodes in Admin > Product Barcodes
4. Track scans in Admin > Analytics

SECURITY NOTES
--------------
- Only scan from original packaging
- Suspicious scans may be flagged
- Each batch is traceable
